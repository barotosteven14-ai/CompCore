HELPFUL REMINDERS

Beginner Tips

01

Don't choose a GPU without considering the CPU.

02

Don't buy a PSU based only on its wattage.

03

Check motherboard compatibility before buying a CPU.

04

Make sure the GPU fits inside the case.

05

Make sure the CPU cooler fits the CPU and case.

06

Leave room for future upgrades.

07

Don't spend the entire budget on one component.

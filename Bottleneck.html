Bottleneck Prevention

A bottleneck happens when one component limits the performance of another component.

CPU Bottleneck

A CPU bottleneck can happen when the processor cannot keep up with the graphics card in certain games or applications.

GPU Bottleneck

A GPU bottleneck happens when the graphics card is the main limit to performance. This is common in graphically demanding games and higher resolutions.

Important

Bottlenecks depend on the game or application, resolution, graphics settings, frame rate target, and workload. There is no single bottleneck percentage that is always accurate.
